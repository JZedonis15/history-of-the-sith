    Origins of the Sith

    The Sith are an ancient order of Force users who embrace the dark side of the Force.Their history stretches back thousands of years before the events of the Star Wars films.

    The Sith Order developed from ancient conflicts between Force users who disagreed about how the Force should be used. While the Jedi valued peace and self-control, the Sith believed that passion and power were the keys to freedom.

    Sith Philosophy

    The Sith believe that emotions such as anger, fear, ambition, and hatred can provide strength through the dark side of the Force.

    The Sith Code

    Peace is a lie, there is only passion.

    Through passion, I gain strength.

    Through strength, I gain power.

    Through power, I gain victory.

    Through victory, my chains are broken.

    The Force shall free me.

    Unlike the Jedi, who seek balance through discipline, the Sith pursue personal power and domination.

    The Rule of Two

    Darth Bane established the Rule of Two after recognizing that constant fighting between Sith weakened their order.

    Under this rule, only two Sith Lords could exist at one time: a master who possessed power and an apprentice who desired to obtain it.

    Purpose of the Rule

    1. Prevent large groups of Sith from destroying each other.
    2. Allow Sith knowledge to pass from master to apprentice.
    3. Strengthen the Sith through competition.
    4. Keep the Sith hidden from the Jedi.

    Famous Sith Lords

    Darth Bane

    Darth Bane was a powerful Sith Lord who created the Rule of Two. His teachings shaped the Sith Order for generations.

    Darth Plagueis

    Darth Plagueis was a Sith Lord fascinated by immortality and the manipulation of life through the Force. He trained Darth Sidious.

    Darth Sidious

    Also known as emperor Palpatine, Darth Sidious manipulated the Galactic Republic and orchestrated the destruction of the Jedi Order.

    His actions led to the creation of the Galactic Empire and the rise of Sith control over the galaxy.

    Darth Vader

    Originally known as Anakin Skywalker, Darth Vader was a Jedi Knight who fell to the dark side.

    Vader became the apprentice of Darth Sidious and helped enforce the rule of the Galactic Empire.

    Darth Maul

    Darth Maul was trained by Darth Sidious and became known for his double-bladed red lightsaber.

    After surviving his defeat on Naboo, Maul built a criminal empire and continued seeking revenge against the Jedi.

    Legacy of the Sith

    The Sith played a major role in shaping galactic history. Their conflicts with the Jedi resulted in wars, political manipulation, and the rise and fall of empires.

    Although individual Sith Lords were defeated, their teachings and influence continued to affect the galaxy for thousands of years.

    Major Events in Sith history

    1. The formation of the ancient Sith order.
    2. The wars between the Jedi and Sith.
    3. Darth Bane establishes the Rule of Two.
    4. Darth Sidious secretly takes control of the Republic
    5. The Jedi Order falls during Order 66.
    6. The Galactic Empire rises under Emperor Palpatine.
    7. Darth Vader turns against Sidious.
    8. The Sith influence continues through later conflicts.

    Did You Know?

    The red color of Sith lightsabers comes from kyber crystals that have been corrupted through a dark side process known as bleeding.
